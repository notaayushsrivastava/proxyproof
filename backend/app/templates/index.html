Vulnerability Audit

Why Legacy Roll-Call Systems Fail

Universities lose thousands of hours and integrity every semester relying on vulnerable 20th-century check-in schemes.

Legacy Vulnerabilities

Traditional QR & Manual Roll Call

  • Static QR Screenshot Sharing: Students take a screenshot of projected QR codes and send them to absent peers over messaging apps.
  • Invasive GPS Tracking: GPS requires location permissions, infringes student privacy, fails indoors, and is easily faked with location spoofing apps.
  • Multi-Phone Mule Logging: One present student carries three smartphones to log in friends sitting in their dorms.
  • Wasted Class Time: Calling 120 names consumes 15 to 20 minutes of crucial instruction every single lecture.
The ProxyProof Standard

Cryptographic Physical Presence

  • 5-Second Cryptographic Expiry: Nonces rotate faster than human reaction time, preventing any screenshot forwarding.
  • Zero GPS Tracking: Uses low-energy radio attenuation indoors with zero global coordinates recorded or requested.
  • Hardware Key Binding: Account credentials cannot be passed to a peer without triggering an administrative device re-verification.
  • Instant Frictionless Check-In: Entire lecture hall verifies in parallel within seconds with zero interruption to teaching.
Key Features

Built for Real University Classrooms

Designed for simplicity, speed, and uncompromising student attendance integrity.

5-Second Fast-Rotating Security Codes

The instructor's phone broadcasts a secure wireless beacon that automatically changes every 5 seconds. Because the code rots almost immediately, students cannot photograph or message it to absent friends outside.

One Student, One Phone

Each student account locks to their personal registered phone. Bringing a friend's device or logging into multiple accounts on one phone is automatically blocked.

Classroom-Range Signal

The app measures physical Bluetooth signal strength to guarantee that the student is actually seated inside the lecture hall, not standing out in the hallway.

Sub-Second Check-Ins

No long queues or broken barcode scanners. An entire lecture hall of 300+ students can check in simultaneously within seconds.

100% Privacy-Preserving

Zero GPS tracking, zero camera harvesting, and zero background monitoring. The app only listens for classroom Bluetooth for the few seconds when you tap Check In.

Simple 3 Steps

How ProxyProof Works

From classroom arrival to recorded attendance in three effortless steps.

01

Professor Starts the Class

The instructor opens ProxyProof on their phone and starts the session. The phone begins broadcasting an invisible, short-range security code that changes every 5 seconds.

02

Student Taps "Check In"

The student opens the app on their phone and taps one button. The phone detects the classroom beacon, verifies that the signal is close enough, and signs with their verified device lock.

03

Confirmed Instantly

The university server verifies the code, marks the student officially present, and updates the professor’s live dashboard in less than a second.
