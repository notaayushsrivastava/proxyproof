2. Academic Integrity & Anti-Proxy Prohibitions

ProxyProof exists to preserve academic fairness and truthful attendance records. Users are strictly prohibited from:

  • Attempting to record attendance on behalf of another individual ("proxy attendance" or "buddy punching").
  • Transmitting, sharing, or intercepting ephemeral cryptographic nonces to facilitate unauthorized check-ins.
  • Binding multiple student accounts to a single mobile device or utilizing emulator environments to fake Bluetooth hardware signatures.
  • Attempting to jam, disrupt, reverse-engineer, or amplify Bluetooth Low Energy signals to forge RSSI proximity values.

Any violation of these prohibitions constitutes a direct breach of academic integrity and will be referred immediately to the University Disciplinary Committee and Academic Dean.
